# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">The Community</span>

The wiki is one part of a larger Slayer Legend community, and most of the knowledge on these pages was worked out in conversation before anyone wrote it down. This section collects the places that conversation happens and the ways the wiki feeds back into it.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Discord</span>

The [community Discord](https://discord.gg/slayerlegendsna2) is where players compare builds, report what changed in a patch, and answer the questions that are too specific for a wiki page - "will this loadout clear Ragnablood at my stats?" is a Discord question, and the answer often ends up improving a wiki page afterwards. It is also the quickest way to reach the wiki's maintainers.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Comments on Pages</span>

Every content page has a comment thread at the bottom. Use it for the things that belong next to the page: a number that disagrees with your game, a strategy the page does not mention, a question the page left open. Comments are public and are moderated - the maintainers can remove anything off-topic or abusive, and a page's thread often turns into its next edit.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Content Creators</span>

The [content creators page](/creators) is where players submit Slayer Legend video guides and streamer channels. Submissions are reviewed before they are listed - if you make videos about the game, or have learned something from one, that page is where to add it. Approved entries appear there with links back to their creators.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Contributing and Recognition</span>

Anyone can edit a page; [Contributing](/meta/contributing) explains how, and the [Guidelines](/meta/guidelines) cover style and the wiki's custom syntax. Contributions are counted on the [contributor highscore](/highscore), and the most active editors are marked with a star wherever their name appears. If you would rather support the wiki than edit it, the [donate](/donate) page explains what donations cover and what supporters get in return.
