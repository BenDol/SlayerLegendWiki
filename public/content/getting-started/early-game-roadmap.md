# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">The Shape of the Early Game</span>

The community draws the line at the **Ether promotion (around stage 340)** - everything before that is "early game," and it mostly plays itself if you keep your stats fed and your promotions taken. That doesn't mean decisions don't matter; it means the game is generous enough to absorb your mistakes. This roadmap covers the milestones in order and the habits worth building. For everything after Ether, [Andy's Progression Guide](/andy/overview-introduction) picks up exactly where this leaves off.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Milestones in Order</span>

**Levels 1-100:** Follow the guide quests, dump gold into ATK, equip every skill you unlock. Your first [promotions](/character/promotions) land here - Bronze is recommended around level 50, Iron around 90. At level 100 you unlock the ability to rename your character.

**Levels 100-250:** Silver promotion around 180, Gold around 300. Somewhere in here you'll hit **stage 120**, and at **level 250** the [Class system](/character/class) unlocks - classes are summoned with<img src="/images/content/goods/Goods_Diamond.png" alt="" class="inline-image" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;width: 30px; height: 30px; margin: -5px 0 0 0;" data-inline="true" />**Diamonds** and act as another permanent ATK/HP multiplier on top of promotions. Combine five of a grade to climb to the next grade.

**Level 400:** The [Training Diary](/character/training-diary) unlocks - three daily sessions with Sepir that convert into Over Points and raised stat caps. From this point on, doing your diary is part of the daily routine. Don't skip it.

**Onward to Ether:** Mithril (recommended ~450), Orichalcum (~600), Arcanite (~700), Adamant (~850), and Ether (~1000) promotions follow. Around this stretch [soul weapons](/equipment/soul-weapons) come online (the first crafts unlock in the Black Forest region, stages 61-80, but the system deepens dramatically later), and dungeon dailies become your main material income.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Where Should Your Diamonds Go?</span>

This is the early-game question, and the community consensus is pretty settled: **weapon summons first.** Weapons are one of your two primary damage sources and the summon pool pays out across your whole account. After weapons, classes are the next priority, then spirits, then accessories. What you should *not* do is gamble diamonds on relic pulls early - relics are a slow-burn system and the same diamonds do far more work in weapon summons. It is tempting. Resist it.

The game showers a surprising amount of free diamonds on you daily through quests, ads, and login rewards - treat them as income to invest, not a windfall to splurge.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Early Skill Picks</span>

You'll refine builds endlessly later, but a reliable early spread: passives **Earth's Will, Iron Will, Speed Sword, Burning Sword** (permanent ATK and attack speed scaling), and actives with wide coverage like **Fulgurous, Red Lightning, Thunderbolt Slash** for farming, swapping toward single-target hitters like **Giga Strike** and **Hellfire Slash** for promotion bosses. Details and full skill data live in the [Skill Database](/database/skills), and you can plan loadouts in the [Skill Builder](/skill-builder).

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Daily Habits That Compound</span>

- Spend your<img src="/images/content/goods/Goods_Wing.png" alt="" class="inline-image" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;width: 30px; height: 30px; margin: -5px 0 0 0;" data-inline="true" />**Glow Feathers** (10 per day from login and quests) on dungeon runs - the Closed Mine's enhance cubes are your equipment progression.
- Use both daily **ad scrolls** during an active farming session - a 300% drop-rate hour is worth planning around.
- Weekend **Hot Time** doubles gold and EXP. Push stages and farm hard on weekends.
- Do the training diary sessions once unlocked. Three a day, every day.

None of these take long, and they compound. The players who pull ahead in this game are not the ones grinding hardest - they're the ones who never miss the five-minute dailies. Be that player.
