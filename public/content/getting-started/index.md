# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Welcome, Slayer</span>

Slayer Legend is a pixel-art idle RPG by GEAR2 where you grow a single sword-swinging character from a nobody in the Beginning Forest into something that one-shots screen-fulls of monsters across 2,000 stages. The core loop is simple - your Slayer fights on their own, you spend what they earn to make them stronger - but underneath that simple loop sits a surprisingly deep stack of systems: promotions, classes, skills, spirits, companions, soul weapons, and more. This section gets you through the front door without wasting your early resources.

If you only read one thing here, read the roadmap. The early game is forgiving, but a few early habits (like where your diamonds go) pay off for months.
