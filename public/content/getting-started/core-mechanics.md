# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">The Combat Loop</span>

Your Slayer walks right and kills what's in front of them. Each stage sends waves of monsters and ends with a boss challenge - beat the boss, advance to the next stage, repeat across 2,000 stages and 30+ regions (see the [stage list](/stages)). If a boss is too strong you stay on the current stage and keep farming it, which is not a failure state: everything the stage drops - gold, EXP, cubes, stones, equipment - keeps flowing while you grow. Pushing and farming are the two modes of the whole game.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Stats Are the Engine</span>

Gold levels your combat stats, and combat stats decide everything. ATK determines your damage, HP and HP Recovery keep you alive against bosses, and the critical stats (Critical %, Critical Damage, and later Death Strike) multiply your output on top. Stat levels run deep - the current cap on main stats is 1,750,000 - so gold never stops being useful. The full breakdown, including growth points and the miscellaneous stats like Luck and Accuracy, lives on the [Stats page](/character/stats).

One thing worth internalizing early: almost every system in this game is, at the end of the day, another multiplier on ATK and HP. Promotions, classes, equipment, spirits, companions - they stack multiplicatively on your base stats. That is why the game keeps feeling fresh: each new system is another lever.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Mana and Skills</span>

Active [skills](/skills) cost MP and run on cooldowns; passive skills are always-on buffs while equipped. Your MP pool recovers over time, which means mana recovery genuinely matters for skill-heavy builds (this is Luna's whole job, if you were wondering about the [companions](/companions)). The Auto toggle fires your actives for you - leave it on unless you are manually timing skills for a boss.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">The Four Elements</span>

Fire, Water, Wind, and Earth run through everything: skills carry an element, attribute damage boosts one element's output, and companions, spirits, and familiars each align to one. You don't need to sweat elemental strategy in the early game - but when you later see "Fire DMG +x%" on a relic or a black orb option, that is what it feeds.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Promotions: The Big Multipliers</span>

Every promotion rank is a one-time boss battle that permanently multiplies your ATK and HP - from Bronze's x2 all the way up to multipliers in the tens of millions at the highest ranks. Promotions are the spine of progression; the stage wall you hit is very often just a promotion you haven't taken yet. Recommended levels and the full multiplier table are on the [Promotions page](/character/promotions).

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Idle Rewards</span>

The game keeps earning while you're away - gold, EXP, enhance cubes, attribute stones, and more, all scaled to your highest stage. Offline earnings are leaner than active play (active farming with ad buffs beats idling by a wide margin), but they are far from nothing, and some materials - enhance cubes especially, plus white souls for [soul weapons](/equipment/soul-weapons) later - accumulate respectably overnight. Check in, collect, spend, push. That's the rhythm.
