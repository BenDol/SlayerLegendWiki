# Contact

The wiki is maintained by players, and the channels below reach us directly. Pick whichever fits - there's no contact form here on purpose, since plain links work on every device and nothing gets lost in a form backend.

## Discord - fastest

Join the community server: **[discord.gg/slayerlegendsna2](https://discord.gg/slayerlegendsna2)**

Maintainers and experienced players are active daily. Best for quick corrections ("this table says X, the game shows Y"), questions a page didn't answer, and anything you want a fast reply on. Most messages get a response the same day.

## GitHub issues - for anything that should be tracked

Open an issue: **[github.com/BenDol/SlayerLegendWiki/issues](https://github.com/BenDol/SlayerLegendWiki/issues)**

Best for tool bugs (a builder miscalculating, a page failing to load), wrong or missing data you can point at specifically, and requests you want a public record of. Include a link to the affected page and what you expected to see. Issues are public, so leave personal details out of them.

## Fix it yourself - for content corrections

Every content page has an **Edit** button. Sign in with GitHub, make the change, and submit - it becomes a pull request that a maintainer reviews before anything goes live. This is usually the quickest route for typos and small factual fixes, and [Contributing](/meta/contributing) walks through it step by step.

## What to get in touch about

- **Corrections** - a stat, cost or drop rate that doesn't match the game.
- **Missing data** - something the wiki should cover and doesn't yet.
- **Tool bugs** - any of the builders or calculators behaving wrongly, on any device.
- **Content removal and copyright** - if you own the rights to material on this site and want it removed, credited differently or changed, tell us through Discord or a GitHub issue and we will act on it promptly. This applies to images, game assets and text alike.
- **Privacy requests** - handled as described in the [privacy policy](/meta/privacy-policy), which lists a dedicated contact route for them.
- **Contributing** - if you'd like to help maintain a section or write guides, say hello on Discord and we'll point you at what needs doing.

## What we can't help with

Anything inside the game itself. Account recovery, lost purchases, crashes and in-game bug reports all belong with the developer through the game's own support channels - we're an independent fan site and have no access to game accounts or servers.
