# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">The Stage Road</span>

The main game is a single long road. Every stage is a set of monster waves capped by a boss challenge: beat the boss and the next stage opens, fail and you keep farming where you are - which is never wasted time, because every reward the game pays scales with your stage number.

The road has a strict shape. Twenty stages make a **chapter**, shown in game as a named region. Inside a chapter, a handful of named **areas** are subdivided into **zones** numbered with Roman numerals - *Beginner's Ground III* is zone III of that area, in the Beginning Forest. Early chapters are patchworks of five or six areas; from roughly chapter 33 onward a chapter is usually a single place running from zone I to zone XX.

The game client currently defines **83 named chapters, covering stages 1 through 1,660**, and carries two further chapter slots that exist but have not been named yet. That ceiling has moved repeatedly - patch notes raised it to 640, then 700, then 740, and added regions at 840 and 900 - so it will very likely move again.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Pushing vs. Farming, In Short</span>

The maths mostly answers this for you.

**Push** as deep as you can clear, because several things key off your record and never go backwards: the Stage Gold multiplier, your daily Black Mana allowance, your auto-hunting rate and your idle soul income. A stage you clear once and abandon still pays you forever.

**Then park** at the lowest stage of the furthest region you have reached, not at the deepest stage you can barely survive. Gold and EXP are paid as a fixed budget *per wave*, so a stage where kills are instant beats a deeper one where you grind - and cubes, stones and equipment are rolled *per kill*, so if you are farming materials rather than gold, take the biggest wave you can still clear quickly. That split is explained properly in [rewards and scaling](/stages/rewards).

When the wall stops moving, it is nearly always a [promotion](/character/promotions), class grade or [skill](/skills) upgrade you are missing - not a need to farm the same stage for another week.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Beyond the Main Road</span>

The **Adventure** menu holds the side content - the Closed Great Mine, Training Cave, Spacetime Rift, Sleeping Flame's Hideout and the Cyclic Forest - each with its own daily entry limits and its own [material rewards](/resources/materials). Only the Cyclic Forest is gated by stage; the rest want character level. A daily lap through them is part of any efficient routine, and the details are on [stage milestones](/stages/milestones).

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Where These Numbers Come From</span>

Region, area and zone names are extracted from the game client's own localization data, so they match what you see on your screen. The per-stage statistics come from the community's datamined stage table, whose figures stay genuine up to **stage 1,376** - past that the source stops producing new values and repeats itself, and recycled numbers are worse than none. Because a chapter is only tabulated when all twenty of its stages are covered, the per-stage tables run to the end of chapter 68, **stage 1,360**. [Stage mechanics](/stages/mechanics) explains this in full, along with the handful of known errors in that source.

If you play deeper than 1,376 and can supply real stage figures, that is the most valuable [contribution](/meta/contributing) this section could receive.
