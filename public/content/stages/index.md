# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">How Stages Work</span>

The main game is a single long road: 2,000 stages, each one a set of monster waves capped by a boss challenge. Beat the boss and you advance; fail and you keep farming where you are, which is never wasted time - every reward the game gives scales with your stage number. Gold per kill, EXP, enhance cube drops, attribute stone chances, even the rarity of equipment drops all climb as you push. Deeper stages also start paying out<img src="/images/content/goods/Goods_Dice_1.png" alt="" class="inline-image" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;width: 30px; height: 30px; margin: -5px 0 0 0;" data-inline="true" />**Dice**, souls for [soul weapons](/equipment/soul-weapons), and eventually a trickle of<img src="/images/content/goods/Goods_Diamond.png" alt="" class="inline-image" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;width: 30px; height: 30px; margin: -5px 0 0 0;" data-inline="true" />**Diamonds** in your idle rewards.

Wave sizes grow too - the first stages throw one monster at a time at you, while stages a few hundred in send packs of eight. This is why area-of-effect skills dominate farming builds, and why your [battle loadout](/battle-loadouts) for pushing a boss looks different from your loadout for farming. Equipment drops start at Common rarity in the opening stages and climb through Great, Rare, and Epic to Legendary-grade drops around stage 500.

A few regions are worth flagging because other systems key off them: the **Black Forest** (61-80) is where the first [soul weapon](/equipment/soul-weapons) becomes craftable, stage **120** in the Land of Desire unlocks the [Class](/character/class) system, and reaching stage **320** at the end of the Lava Plateau opens soul weapon engraving.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Pushing vs. Farming</span>

The eternal question: park or push? The math mostly answers it for you. Because rewards scale with stage number, you always want to farm at the highest stage you can clear *quickly and safely* - a stage where kills are instant beats a higher one where you grind through packs slowly. Push whenever your [promotions](/character/promotions) and stats let you, then settle one comfortable notch back and let the [farming loop](/resources/farming) do its work. When the wall stops moving, it's nearly always a promotion, class grade, or skill upgrade you're missing - not a need to farm the same stage for another week.

Beyond the main road, the **Adventure** menu holds the side content - dungeons like the Closed Mine and Training Cave with their daily entry limits, and boss encounters like the Shelter of Sleeping Flame that feed [latent power](/character/latent-power). Those have their own economies and their own [material rewards](/resources/materials), and a daily lap through them is part of any efficient routine.
