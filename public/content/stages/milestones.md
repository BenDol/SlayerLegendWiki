# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">What Unlocks at Which Stage</span>

Stage number is one of the two progression currencies in Slayer Legend - character level is the other - and a surprising amount of the game is locked behind it. These are the discrete unlocks, in order.

| Stage | Unlocks | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Every 5** | <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BenDol/SlayerLegendCDN/main/user-content/images/characters/2026/01/0d56a0e813ef4d4794b0f719473e88d5.png" alt="" class="inline-image" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;width: 24px; height: 24px; margin: -4px 0 0 0;" data-inline="true" /> **Memory Essence** | Not a one-time unlock - every fifth stage cleared pays essence, which feeds the [Memory Tree](/character/class) and raises resource gain across the game |
| **40** | **Emergency Request** | A one-time, thirty-tier reward chain. Non-repeatable once completed, and it pays out in diamonds, emeralds, attribute stones, feathers and summon tickets |
| **80** | **Soul Weapon crafting** | The last stage of the Black Forest. New soul weapon tiers then appear roughly every twenty stages from here onward |
| **100** | **Cyclic Forest** dungeon | Also called the Forest of Circulation. Rewards mana crystals; three free entries a day |
| **120** | **Class system** | Also requires character **level 250**. The single most important early goal in the game - clear 120 and the whole class and awakening tree opens |
| **300** | **Spirits** | The [spirit](/spirits) system opens on clearing stage 300 |
| **320** | **Soul Weapon Engraving** | Tied to crafting a stage-320-tier soul weapon or better |

Beyond these, individual events and cosmetics carry their own gates - the client has generic strings for *"Opens upon clearing Stage N"* and *"Can be equipped after clearing stage N"* - so seasonal content routinely asks for a specific stage without it being a permanent system unlock.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">What Scales With Your Deepest Clear</span>

Just as important, and much easier to miss: several systems do not unlock at a stage, they simply **get better the deeper you have ever been**. The client says so explicitly.

- **Auto-hunting rewards** - *"the auto hunting reward is determined by the highest reached stage."* Your offline income is set by your record, not by where you are parked.
- **Black Mana** - *"the amount of Black Mana that can be obtained per day increases according to the highest stage cleared."* A daily cap that rises purely with depth. See [black orbs](/equipment/black-orbs).
- **Battlefield EXP** - *"gain more EXP on the battlefield with each stage you clear."*
- **The Stage Gold multiplier** - a permanent gold bonus that steps up roughly every ten stages from stage 90 onward, reaching ×26.5 by stage 1,376. See [stage rewards](/stages/rewards).

This is why pushing is worth doing even into stages you have no intention of farming. A stage you clear once and walk away from still permanently raises your idle income, your daily Black Mana and every gold reward you will ever collect.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Milestones In the Stage Table Itself</span>

Separately from unlocks, the stage data has its own thresholds - the points where a number stops moving for good. These are the ones worth knowing, because past each one there is no point waiting for it to improve:

| Stage | What changes |
|---|---|
| 2 | Diamonds start appearing in idle rewards |
| 28 | Dice start appearing in idle rewards |
| 141 | Souls start appearing in idle rewards |
| **221** | **Equipment drops reach Legendary 3 and never improve again** |
| 321 | Idle diamonds cap at 6 |
| 441 | Enemy attack speed locks at 0.6 |
| 501 | Boss health locks at exactly 500× a normal monster |
| 521 | Dice per clear cap at 20 |
| 827 | Attribute stone chance caps at 1.725% |
| 1,001 | Equipment drop chance locks at 0.118% |

Full detail on all of these is on the [rewards and scaling](/stages/rewards) and [drops](/stages/drops) pages.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Not Stage-Gated: the Level Track</span>

This trips up a lot of players. Most of the **Adventure** menu is gated by **character level**, not by stage, so grinding stages will not open it and vice versa.

| Content | Gate | Entry limit | Rewards |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Closed Great Mine** | Character level 200 | Costs feathers; 10 runs base, up to 30 | Gold, EXP, diamonds, emeralds, attribute stones |
| **Training Cave** | Character level ~245-250 | 5 per day (2 free + 3 paid) | EXP, rare class item drops |
| **Spacetime Rift** | Character level 280 | 3 free, more with purple feathers | Enhancement cubes, plus its own ranking track |
| **Sleeping Flame's Hideout** | Character level ~265-300 | 3 free per day | Dragon's Power, Dragon's Flame |
| **Cyclic Forest** | **Stage 100** | 3 free, more with cyan feathers | Mana crystals |
| **Guild** | Silver promotion (around level 180) | - | Guild quests, Divine Beast, seasonal events |

Only the Cyclic Forest is stage-gated. Everything else on that menu wants levels, which come from [EXP farming](/progression/leveling) and [promotions](/character/promotions) rather than from pushing depth.

> Sources disagree slightly on two of these - the Training Cave is quoted as both 245 and 250, and Sleeping Flame's Hideout as both 265 and 300, most likely because the figures were recorded from different game versions. Both ranges are given rather than picking one.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">How Far the Road Goes</span>

The stage ceiling has moved repeatedly, and each raise is documented in the official patch notes:

| Patch | Ceiling |
|---|---|
| v408 | Stage packages up to stage 300 |
| v415 | **640** |
| v420.2.1 | **700** |
| v420.5.9 | **740** |
| v420.6.4 | Regions added at **840** |
| v420.6.7 | Regions added at **900** |

The current game client goes considerably further than any of those notes describe: it defines **83 named chapters covering stages 1 to 1,660**, plus two further unnamed chapter slots waiting to be filled. Whether every one of those chapters is live in your build depends on your version, but the names are in the client, and this wiki lists all of them - see [the full stage list](/stages).

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">A Sensible Order to Chase Them</span>

1. **Stage 40** for the Emergency Request chain - it is free diamonds and it is quick.
2. **Stage 80** for soul weapon crafting, then keep clearing for blueprints.
3. **Stage 120 plus level 250** for the class system. This is the real early-game goal; everything is easier afterwards.
4. **Stage 100** for the Cyclic Forest, which will happen on the way.
5. **Stage 300** for spirits, and **320** for engraving - by this point rewards are large enough that the game changes character.
6. After that, there are no more system unlocks on the road. Everything past stage 320 is about scale: gold multipliers, soul income, and the daily caps that rise with your deepest clear.
