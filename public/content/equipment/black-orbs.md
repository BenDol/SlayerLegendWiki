# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Black Orb Overview</span>

Black Orbs are late-game equipment content built around **elemental (attribute) damage**. Where your [weapon](/equipment/weapons) multiplies raw ATK, Black Orb equipment multiplies your fire, water, wind, and earth damage - the layer of your damage formula that skills, spirits, and companions all feed into. The system arrived in a later update and sits firmly in the endgame: by the time the game hands it to you, you'll have the [soul weapon](/equipment/soul-weapons) and awakening systems running already, and Black Orbs will feel like a natural next floor on the tower.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">How It Works</span>

Black Orb equipment is summoned with<img src="/images/content/goods/Goods_BlackMana.png" alt="" class="inline-image" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;width: 30px; height: 30px; margin: -2px 0 0 0;" data-inline="true" />**Black Mana**. Each piece follows the familiar structure with an elemental twist:

- The **main option** matches the equipment's attribute and raises that element's damage.
- **Additional options** roll smaller amplification lines - elemental damage boosts in the 2% to 7% range across the four elements (plus attack-speed style lines) - with up to **four additional option lines** depending on the equipment's grade.

Higher-grade pieces mean more lines and bigger rolls, so the chase here is the same as everywhere else in the game: grade first, then good rolls on the lines.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Awakening and Black Pieces</span>

The awakening layer ties the Black Orb system back into the accessory line, and it works like this:

1. **Only Mythic grade or higher equipment can attempt awakening.**
2. Awakening consumes **black pieces**, which you obtain by awakening the **Orb of Hope** - the endgame [accessory](/equipment/accessories).
3. If you want your black piece back, a reset costs<img src="/images/content/goods/Goods_Diamond.png" alt="" class="inline-image" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;width: 30px; height: 30px; margin: -5px 0 0 0;" data-inline="true" />**3,000 Diamonds** - and note that **replacing the equipment resets its awakening automatically**, so awaken the piece you intend to keep, not the piece you have.

That last rule deserves the emphasis: awakening is an investment in a specific item. Sinking black pieces into gear you're about to out-grade is the expensive mistake this system offers, and the 3,000-diamond reset is the tax for making it. Awaken late, awaken deliberately.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Where It Fits in Your Progression</span>

Elemental damage percentage is a multiplier on damage you already do, which means Black Orbs reward accounts that are already strong - they amplify a big base rather than create one. Practical implications:

- Prioritize the element your build actually uses. If your skill loadout leans fire, fire attribute lines are worth more to you than a rainbow of small ones.
- [Relics](/equipment/relics) have elemental damage lines too (Silence Flame, Abyss's Water Drop, Eye of Typoon, Emperor Ring) - your element choice should be consistent across systems for the multipliers to stack meaningfully.
- Black Mana income is limited, so treat summons here the way you treated early weapon summons: steady investment, no impulse spending.

This page covers the system as the community currently understands it - Black Orb content is still evolving with game updates, and if you're playing at this depth, corrections and current numbers are exactly the kind of [contribution](/meta/contributing) the wiki needs.
