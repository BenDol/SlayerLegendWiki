# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Relics Overview</span>

Relics are the quiet system of the equipment family: passive stat boosters (twelve in the wiki's current data) that don't occupy an equipment slot, don't drop from monsters, and don't demand build decisions. You acquire them through the **shop** and level them up over time, and each level nudges its stat a little higher - permanently, passively, account-wide. No single relic changes your game. All twelve, leveled patiently over months, add up to a very real chunk of power.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">The Twelve Relics</span>

| Relic | Buff |
|----------|----------|
| **Strength Gloves** | Extra DMG |
| **Hunter's Eye** | Critical Damage |
| **HP Ring** | Extra HP |
| **Recovery Totem** | Extra HP Recovery |
| **Bracelet of Speed** | Attack Speed |
| **Lucky Pendent** | Monster Defeat Gold |
| **Focus Ring** | Accuracy Rate |
| **Invisible Cloak** | Dodge Rate |
| **Silence Flame** | Fire DMG |
| **Abyss's Water Drop** | Water DMG |
| **Eye of Typoon** | Wind DMG |
| **Emperor Ring** | Earth DMG |

*"Lucky Pendent" and "Eye of Typoon" are the game's own spellings, not ours - they're listed here as they appear in-game so you can find them. If the game has added relics beyond these twelve, [add them](/meta/contributing).*

The spread tells you the design intent: four combat fundamentals (damage, crit, HP, recovery), a set of utility lines (attack speed, gold, accuracy, dodge), and one relic per element mirroring the four attributes that run through [skills](/skills), [spirits](/spirits), and [Black Orbs](/equipment/black-orbs). The elemental relics scale harder at high levels than the flat utility ones, which fits their role as late-game multipliers on an established elemental build.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Leveling Relics</span>

Relic upgrades are attempt-based, and here's the catch: **success rates fall as the relic's level climbs** - dropping under 50% at higher levels. Early levels are cheap and reliable; late levels are a war of attrition. That failure curve is the system's real cost, and it's why relics reward a drip-feed approach: put spare resources in regularly, shrug off the failures, and let the months do the work. Trying to force a high-level relic upgrade in one sitting is how players burn resources they needed elsewhere.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">How Much Should You Invest?</span>

Early game: honestly, almost nothing. The community's diamond priority list puts relics dead last - weapon summons, classes, spirits, and accessories all convert diamonds into power far more efficiently, and the standing advice for new players is *don't gamble diamonds on relics early*. It's not that relics are bad; it's that everything else is better first, and relics will still be there (with the same slow curve) when your other systems mature.

Mid to late game the calculus shifts. Once your weapon is climbing the Mythic ladder and your class is fused high, relic levels become one of the few places passive power is still cheap - especially **Strength Gloves** and **Hunter's Eye** for damage-focused accounts, **Lucky Pendent** for farming economies, and your build's elemental relic once your attribute damage stack is established. At that stage, a relic habit (top up levels whenever resources allow, stop when the failure rate stings) is exactly the right amount of attention. Set it, drip it, forget it.
