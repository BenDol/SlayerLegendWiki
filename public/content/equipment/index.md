# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Equipment Overview</span>

Equipment in Slayer Legend is not one system - it's five or six stacked on top of each other, unlocking in layers as you progress. The core pair is simple: a **weapon** (your main damage multiplier) and an **accessory** (your survivability and utility multiplier). Then the game keeps adding floors to the building: enhancement to level them, fusion to climb rarities, **soul weapons** wielded alongside your normal weapon, **black orbs** for elemental damage, and **relics** quietly buffing from the sidelines. Each layer is its own page in this section.

One concept carries across all of it and is worth learning immediately: most equipment has both an **equip effect** (active while worn) and an **owned effect** (a smaller passive bonus you keep merely for having the item). This is why summoning duplicate gear is never wasted - your whole collection works for you, not just the pieces you wear.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Priorities, Briefly</span>

If you're wondering where to focus: weapons first, always - they're one of your two primary damage sources (skills being the other) and diamond weapon summons are the best-value spend in the game. Accessories matter but can wait; a good Legendary accessory carries you a very long way before upgrading it becomes urgent. Soul weapons and black orbs arrive on their own schedule as you push stages. And relics are a slow drip you set up once and top off occasionally - useful, never urgent.
