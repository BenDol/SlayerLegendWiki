# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Tools & Calculators</span>

Every tool on this page is free, runs right in your browser, and is built on the same game data as the wiki's reference pages - so what you plan in a tool matches what you read in the guides. Sign in with GitHub to save your builds and share them with other players; without signing in you can still experiment freely.

The honest pitch for why these exist: Slayer Legend's build decisions are expensive to experiment with in-game. Dice rolls, Chaos Soul rerolls, skill respecs - the game charges you to find out whether an idea was good. The tools let you find out for free first.

## <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">📖 [Battle Loadouts](/battle-loadouts)</span>

The everything-planner: assemble a complete setup - skills, skill stones, spirits, familiars, soul weapons, engravings - as one shareable loadout. This is the tool for "here's my whole build, what should I change?" conversations, and for browsing what stronger players are actually running.

## <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">⚔️ [Skill Builder](/skill-builder)</span>

Plan your skill bar against the full [skill database](/database/skills) - grades, MP costs, cooldowns, and scaling all visible while you build. Ideal for designing your farming and boss presets before spending anything in-game.

## <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">🔮 [Spirit Builder](/spirit-builder)</span>

Configure your [spirit](/spirits) lineup - compare the farming set against the boss set, see every spirit's skill and ranges, and share configurations. Pairs with the [Spirit Viewer](/spirits/viewer) if you want to watch your picks animate through their evolution stages.

## <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">👹 [Familiar Builder](/familiar-builder)</span>

Build familiar setups - the twelve base familiars and the sixteen prime familiars formed by combining three base familiars from different categories - with star progression and category bonuses laid out visually.

## <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">🗡️ [Soul Weapon Engraving](/soul-weapon-engraving)</span>

The grid puzzle, solved for free. Lay out [engraving pieces](/equipment/soul-weapons) on real soul weapon grids, rotate and rearrange until completion clicks, and share layouts - instead of gambling Chaos Souls on experiments in-game. If you only ever use one tool here, engraving planning is the one that pays for itself fastest.

## <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">💎 [Skill Stone Builder](/skill-stone-builder)</span>

Plan your [refinement](/skills/skill-refinement) setup - Cooldown, Time, and Heat stones across your skill bar, with tiers and elements - and compare arrangements before committing stones.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Saving and Sharing</span>

Signed-in users get saved builds under [My Collections](/my-collections), and any build can be shared as a link or embedded in wiki pages and guides - which is exactly how the community's reference builds stay current. Built something that outperforms the standard advice? Share it, then [write it up](/meta/contributing). That's how the meta moves.
