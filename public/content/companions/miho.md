# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Miho</span>

<img src="/images/content/companions/miho/ninja_01.png" alt="Miho" class="inline-image" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle; width: 96px; image-rendering: pixelated; margin: 0 0.5em 0 0;" data-inline="true" />Miho is the **Fire** companion - a shadow-stepping looter with a split personality. Half her kit is survival (damage resistance and dodge, both checked against enemy level, both increasingly precious as you push above your weight). The other half is **Gold Rush**, and let's be honest, Gold Rush is why people love her. A percentage boost to *total gold* in a game where gold levels every combat stat isn't a farming perk - it's compound interest on your entire account. Every gold coin you'll ever earn, slightly bigger, forever.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Skills</span>

| Skill | Effect | Max Lv |
|----------|----------|----------|
| **Shadow Step** | Damage Resistance | 100 |
| **Shadow Dance** | Dodge | 100 |
| **Gold Rush** | Total Gold | 100 |

Her locked later skills complete the pattern: a boost to the **Red [soul weapon](/equipment/soul-weapons) completion effect**, bonus **damage against Devournists** (her late-game boss family), and a boost to **Fire [spirit](/spirits) stats** - a natural pairing with the Fire farming spirits.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Playing Around Miho</span>

- **Gold Rush first, and it isn't close.** It pays out on every kill you'll ever make. The [gold guide](/resources/gold) covers how it stacks with Luck, Extra Monster Gold rolls, the spirit Todd, and the Lucky Pendent relic - the full farming economy is a stack of multipliers, and Miho holds one of the big ones.
- **The defensive pair matters at walls.** When a promotion boss or deep stage is killing you rather than out-lasting you, Shadow Step and Shadow Dance are levels you can buy with stones instead of gold.
- **Advancement battles:** fought *without Fire skills* - inconvenient if your damage bar leans Hellfire Slash and friends. Keep an off-element loadout ready.
- **[Promotion options](/companions/promotion-option):** Extra ATK remains the default roll, though dedicated farming accounts have a case for Monster Gold rolls on her pages. [Companion Builds](/companions/companion-builds) walks through the tradeoff.
