# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Luna</span>

<img src="/images/content/companions/luna/mage_01.png" alt="Luna" class="inline-image" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle; width: 96px; image-rendering: pixelated; margin: 0 0.5em 0 0;" data-inline="true" />Luna is the **Water** companion - a trainee magician, and the engine room of every skill-focused build in the game. Her kit reads modest next to [Zeke's](/companions/zeke) raw power: mana recovery, a bigger mana pool, and skill damage. But think about what actually limits a skill build. It isn't skill levels - it's the MP bar. A bar full of leveled actives that sits empty half the fight is a half-strength build, and Luna is the difference between "my skills fire when the MP allows" and "my skills fire on cooldown." Then Wisdom of War multiplies every one of those casts on top. Quiet kit, loud results.

Her locked later skills mirror the others': a boost to the **Blue [soul weapon](/equipment/soul-weapons) completion effect**, bonus **damage against Origin of Chaos** (her late-game boss family), and a boost to **Water [spirit](/spirits) stats**.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Playing Around Luna</span>

- **Wisdom of War is the headline level.** Skill damage multiplies your entire active bar - for a build built around Fulgurous spam or big Legendary nukes, it's among the best companion levels in the game.
- **The mana pair scales with your ambitions.** Early builds barely notice MP; late builds running expensive actives back-to-back live and die on recovery. If you're using Meditation to force casts, Luna's mana kit is telling you it wants levels.
- **She defines the farming rhythm.** Sustainable AoE spam across eight-monster waves is a mana problem before it's a damage problem - which is why farming builds that look identical on paper perform differently depending on Luna investment.
- **Advancement battles:** fought *without Water skills*. Plan the loadout before the fight, not during.
- **[Promotion options](/companions/promotion-option):** Extra ATK by default, but this is the one companion where a mana recovery roll or two is openly recommended when your build runs dry - the standard advice, straight from the [promotion options guide](/companions/promotion-option).
