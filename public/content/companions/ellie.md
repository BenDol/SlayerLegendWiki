# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Ellie</span>

<img src="/images/content/companions/ellie/archer_01.png" alt="Ellie" class="inline-image" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle; width: 96px; image-rendering: pixelated; margin: 0 0.5em 0 0;" data-inline="true" />Ellie is the **Wind** companion - an elf archer, and the offense *enabler* of the four. She doesn't carry your damage the way [Zeke](/companions/zeke) does; she makes your damage land. Accuracy is her signature, and if you've ever watched your Slayer whiff attacks against higher-level enemies (accuracy is checked against the enemy's level - see [Stats](/character/stats)), you already understand why she earns her keep. Her enemy HP reduction is the quieter luxury: every fight starts partly won.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Skills</span>

| Skill | Effect | Max Lv |
|----------|----------|----------|
| **Intensive Fire** | Accuracy | 100 |
| **Blessing of Forest** | Extra ATK | 100 |
| **Song of Elf** | Reduce Enemy HP | 100 |

Her three later skills unlock as she develops, and they show where the game wants her late: a boost to the **Green [soul weapon](/equipment/soul-weapons) completion effect**, bonus **damage against Destructionists** (one of the late-game boss families), and a boost to **Wind [spirit](/spirits) stats**. If your spirit lineup leans Wind, Ellie's late kit multiplies it.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Playing Around Ellie</span>

- **Level Blessing of Forest and Intensive Fire first.** Extra ATK is always live value, and accuracy pays off exactly when you're pushing stages above your weight - which is always.
- **Song of Elf shines against bosses.** Shaving a slice off a promotion boss's HP bar is worth more than it reads - promotion fights are timed, and every percent gone is time bought.
- **Advancement battles:** remember you fight *without Wind skills* to advance her. Keep a non-Wind loadout saved before you need it.
- **[Promotion options](/companions/promotion-option):** the standard advice applies - fill her pages with Extra ATK rolls, with a mana roll or two if your build runs dry (details in [Companion Builds](/companions/companion-builds)).

Like every companion page on the wiki, current skill values at each level are the community's job to keep fresh - if you have her sheet in front of you, [contribute the numbers](/meta/contributing).
