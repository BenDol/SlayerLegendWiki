# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Why Levels Matter</span>

Your character level is quieter than promotions or equipment - no flashy multiplier attached - but it gates a surprising amount of the game. Levels grant growth points to allocate across your [stats](/character/stats), promotions list recommended levels, and several whole systems simply will not open until you hit their level requirement. EXP is the slow current underneath everything else; you want it flowing at all times.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Where EXP Comes From</span>

- **Killing monsters** - the baseline. EXP per kill scales steeply with stage, which is the main reason pushing stages beats over-farming low ones.
- **Idle rewards** - the game keeps earning EXP while you're away, scaled to your highest stage.
- **Extra EXP bonuses** - an Extra EXP roll on your [promotion abilities](/character/stats) or companion [promotion options](/companions/promotion-option), and the spirit **Luga** (10-80% chance to double EXP gains) all compound your rate. Small percentages, huge lifetime value.
- **The training dummy** - once the [Training Diary](/character/training-diary) unlocks at level 400, finishing your session against Sepir lets you keep training against the dummy for straight EXP, up to three times daily.
- **Hot Time** - weekend events double gold and EXP. If you're going to push levels hard, do it on a weekend.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Milestones Worth Knowing</span>

| Level | What Unlocks |
|----------|----------|
| **100** | Character rename becomes available. |
| **250** | The [Class](/character/class) system (together with reaching stage 120) - a major permanent multiplier. |
| **400** | The [Training Diary](/character/training-diary) - daily sessions with Sepir granting Over Points and raised stat caps. |
| **Every level** | Growth points to allocate to combat and miscellaneous stats. |
| **Every 100 levels (post-400)** | Additional Over Points and higher Over Point caps via the diary. |

Promotion recommended levels also march upward in lockstep - roughly level 50 for Bronze, 90 for Iron, 180 for Silver, 300 for Gold, 450 for Mithril, and climbing to 1000 for Ether and 3000 for Gigarock. The [Promotions page](/character/promotions) has the complete table.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Leveling Efficiently</span>

Early on, don't overthink it - EXP arrives as a by-product of playing. The optimization conversation starts mid-game, and it comes down to a handful of habits:

1. **Farm at your highest comfortable stage.** EXP per kill scales with stage number, so a stage you clear quickly and safely beats a higher one where you struggle. Kill speed times EXP per kill is the actual number you're maximizing.
2. **Stack your EXP modifiers before long sessions.** Hot Time weekend + ad scroll buffs + Luga equipped + Extra EXP rolls, then farm actively. The multiplication across all of them is dramatic compared to naked idling.
3. **Never waste dummy sessions.** Post-400, the three daily dummy trainings are free EXP that doesn't depend on your farming stage at all.
4. **Let idle time work at height.** Before logging off, make sure you're parked at your highest farmable stage - idle EXP is calculated from it.

The honest summary: leveling in Slayer Legend rewards patience and stacking, not grinding heroics. Set up your multipliers, park yourself high, show up for your dailies... and the levels take care of themselves.
