# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Why Gold Never Stops Mattering</span>

<img src="/images/content/goods/Goods_Gold.png" alt="" class="inline-image" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;width: 30px; height: 30px; margin: -5px 0 0 0;" data-inline="true" />**Gold** has one overwhelming sink: your [combat stats](/character/stats). ATK, HP, HP Recovery, and the critical family all level with gold, the caps run to 1,750,000 levels, and costs scale relentlessly - which means gold demand outgrows gold income for the entire life of your account. There is no "enough." Every promotion multiplies the value of stats, stats cost gold, and so the whole game quietly reduces to a gold engine with a sword attached.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">The Multiplier Stack</span>

Base gold per kill scales with your stage - steeply. Deep stages don't pay 20% more than shallow ones, they pay orders of magnitude more, which is why stage pushing *is* gold farming. On top of that base, the game offers a remarkable tower of multipliers, and serious farmers stack all of them:

- **Luck (LUK)** - the [miscellaneous stat](/character/stats) that directly increases gold from kills. Cheap early levels, always worth keeping current.
- **Extra Monster Gold rolls** - on your [Slayer promotion abilities](/character/stats) (3-40% per slot by rarity) and optionally on [companion promotion options](/companions/promotion-option).
- **Miho's Gold Rush** - the [companion](/companions/miho) skill boosting *total* gold. One of the big levers; level it.
- **Todd's Gold Rumble** - the [spirit](/spirits/information) granting a 10-80% chance of double gold on every acquisition. The single most famous farming spirit in the game.
- **Lucky Pendent** - the [relic](/equipment/relics) adding monster-defeat gold (yes, that's the in-game spelling), drip-leveled over months.
- **Extra Gold engraving pieces** - the line-shaped [soul weapon](/equipment/soul-weapons) gems, worth slotting in dedicated farming grids.
- **Skill mastery gold nodes** - post-Ether, the [mastery tree](/skills/skill-mastery) sells permanent extra gold. Buy it.
- **Gold ads** - the daily ad-reward gold claims (several per day, scaling with progression). Free money for thirty seconds of tolerance.
- **Hot Time** - weekend events with **2x gold**. Plan your heavy farming sessions around them, seriously - a Saturday of stacked farming outearns a scattered week.

Each layer multiplies the others. A player with Todd, Miho, LUK, gold rolls, and a Hot Time weekend isn't earning 20% more than baseline - they're earning several times more, on every kill, thousands of kills per hour. This is the compounding that separates accounts.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Where to Farm</span>

The rule from the [farming guide](/resources/farming) applies doubly to gold: **farm the highest stage you clear instantly.** Gold per kill rises with stage, but kills per minute collapse when you're grinding through packs slowly - the product of the two is what fills your wallet. If you've just pushed a wall, step back a stage or two for farming sessions; if your "comfortable" stage hasn't moved in a while, that's a [promotion](/character/promotions) or upgrade overdue, and fixing it raises your entire gold curve at once.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Spending It Well</span>

Mostly: spend it, constantly, on stats. Gold hoarded is gold shrinking - every hour of progression inflates costs and income together, so banked gold buys fewer levels tomorrow than today. Keep ATK leading (it drives stage pushing, which drives income), keep HP and recovery close enough to survive bosses, and feed the crit stats as they unlock their multipliers. The one nuance: before a promotion attempt, a focused dump into the exact stats the fight demands (survival, usually) beats spreading evenly. Otherwise - earn, spend, push, repeat. The engine only works if you keep turning it.
