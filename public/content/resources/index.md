# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Resources Overview</span>

Slayer Legend runs on an economy of a dozen-plus currencies, and each one is a bottleneck for something specific. Gold feeds stats, cubes feed equipment, stones feed companions, souls feed soul weapons - and your progression at any moment is usually limited by exactly one of them. Knowing which one, and where it comes from, is half of playing this game well. This section is the map.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">One Principle for All of It</span>

Nearly every resource in this game arrives as a *rate*, not a windfall - so the question is never "how do I get 10,000 cubes" but "what's my cubes-per-day, and which multipliers raise it." Stage height raises every rate at once (rewards scale with your highest stage), buffs and Hot Time multiply the rates temporarily, and the daily-limited sources (feathers, ads, diary sessions) are rate you either claim or lose. Push high, stack multipliers, never skip dailies. Everything else in this section is elaboration on that sentence.
