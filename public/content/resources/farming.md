# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Farming Is the Game</span>

Strip away the systems and Slayer Legend is a farming game: everything - [gold](/resources/gold), EXP, [cubes, stones, souls](/resources/materials) - flows from kills, and every upgrade exists to make the kills faster and deeper. So "farming strategy" isn't a side topic; it's the operating manual. The good news is that efficient farming comes down to four decisions, and none of them are complicated.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Decision One: Where to Park</span>

**Farm the highest stage you clear instantly.** Rewards scale hard with stage number, but so does enemy toughness - and the moment your kills stop being instant, your rewards-per-minute fall off a cliff even as rewards-per-kill rise. The sweet spot is one or two stages below your pushing wall: monsters evaporate, waves cycle fast, and every reward stream runs at its maximum sustainable rate. Park there, and re-park higher every time you push. (Parking high also matters when you log off - idle rewards are calculated from your stage.)

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Decision Two: When to Farm Actively</span>

Offline income is real but reduced - fine for<img src="/images/content/goods/EnchantCube 1_1.png" alt="" class="inline-image" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;width: 26px; height: 26px; margin: -2px 0 0 0;" data-inline="true" />**cubes** and white souls, weak for gold. Active play with buffs stacked is where the real numbers happen, so the skill is *concentrating* your active time:

- **Ad scrolls** - two per day, one hour each, a 300% drop-rate buff. These are your prime farming windows; don't burn them while doing dailies, burn them parked and killing.
- **Gold ads** - several per day, scaling rewards. Free income for a few seconds of ad tolerance.
- **Hot Time** - weekends, 2x gold and EXP. Stack a weekend Hot Time with ad scrolls and a full multiplier setup, and one session outproduces days of casual play. This is the single biggest scheduling trick in the game.
- Some players go further and keep the game running on a spare device or emulator during the day. It's an idle game; it rewards exactly this kind of low-effort scheming.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Decision Three: The Farming Loadout</span>

Farming wants wide, fast, cheap damage - a different animal from your boss build, which is why skill presets exist. The community's farming core: **Fulgurous** (the half-second-cooldown charge that anchors everything), **Red Lightning**, **Thunderbolt Slash**, and **Flame Wave** for coverage, with **Meditation** recycling cooldowns and **Agile** keeping you moving between packs. Passives as always: Earth's Will, Speed Sword, Iron Will and friends.

Then the economy layer on top: [spirits](/spirits/information) **Todd** (2x gold chance), **Luga** (2x EXP chance), **Zappy** (executes wounded monsters), **Mum** and **Radon** (kill speed); [Miho's](/companions/miho) **Gold Rush** leveled; Extra Gold [engraving pieces](/equipment/soul-weapons) in a farming grid if you're deep enough. Build it once in the [Skill Builder](/skill-builder) and [Spirit Builder](/spirit-builder), save the presets, and swapping becomes a ten-second ritual before each session.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Decision Four: The Daily Lap</span>

The daily-limited sources don't stack up - claim them or lose them. The lap: spend your ten<img src="/images/content/goods/Goods_Wing.png" alt="" class="inline-image" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;width: 26px; height: 26px; margin: -2px 0 0 0;" data-inline="true" />**Glow Feathers** on dungeon runs (Closed Mine for cubes, Training Cave as unlocked), run your three [Training Diary](/character/training-diary) sessions, collect login rewards and gold ads, clear daily quests. Fifteen minutes, and it's most of the compounding in your account.

That's the whole doctrine, honestly: park high, concentrate your buffed hours, run the right loadout, never miss the lap. Everything else is patience - and the occasional deeply satisfying promotion that makes the numbers jump.
