# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Andy's Progression Guide</span>

This section holds the wiki's longest-running piece of community strategy writing: a progression guide by Andy, a player with three years in Slayer Legend across two servers and one of the wiki's most active contributors. The [Overview & Introduction](/andy/overview-introduction) is his own text - the philosophy of playing efficiently as a free or light-spending player, and the point in the game (the Ether promotion, around stage 340) where a guide starts to matter.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">What Is Here, and What Isn't</span>

Only the introduction is published under Andy's name. The stage-by-stage advice that a guide like this implies - what to do between Ether and Black Mythril, which multiplier to chase at Dragonos, how to spend dice at Gigarock - is written into the wiki's own pages instead, organised by the promotion that gates each stretch. That was a deliberate choice: the wiki's pages can be corrected by anyone the moment a patch moves a number, while a single author's guide cannot, and the two would otherwise drift apart.

So read this section as a starting point rather than a self-contained walkthrough. Andy's introduction tells you *how to think* about progression; the pages it points at tell you *what to do*, band by band.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Reading Order</span>

If you are below stage 340, start with the [Early Game Roadmap](/getting-started/early-game-roadmap) - Andy skips the early game on purpose because it rarely needs a guide, and that page covers it milestone by milestone. From Ether onward, the [Promotions](/character/promotions) page is the spine: it explains the three phases of the promotion ladder and what to check when a promotion battle will not fall. Around it sit the pages that matter for each band - [Skill Mastery](/skills/skill-mastery) once Ether unlocks it, [Latent Power](/character/latent-power) when Dragonos opens awakening, [Soul Weapons](/equipment/soul-weapons) and [Relics](/equipment/relics) through the middle promotions, [Black Orbs](/equipment/black-orbs) and [Class](/character/class) awakenings late. The per-stage numbers for every one of those stretches are on the [Stages](/stages) pages, and the daily routine Andy refers to is on [Farming Strategies](/resources/farming).

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Contributing to This Section</span>

Andy's introduction is his text and is not edited for content. Everything it links to is ordinary wiki content: if you reach a stage band and find the advice thin, or a number that no longer matches the game, [edit the page](/meta/contributing) - a worked example of a promotion attempt, with the build and account state that made it succeed or fail, is worth more here than any amount of general description.
