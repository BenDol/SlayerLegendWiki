# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Training Diary Overview</span>
**This content unlocks when your character reaches level 400 and allows you to gain additional growth points to level up your stats. This is done by obtaining<img src="/images/content/items/ui_item_Exp_OverPoint_1.png" alt="" class="inline-image" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;width: 30px; height: 30px; margin: -2px 0 0 0;" data-inline="true" /><span class="text-purple-600 dark:text-purple-400">over points</span> every 100 levels and completing training with Sepir which increases the current level cap of your stats in the growth points section.**
<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BenDol/SlayerLegendCDN/main/user-content/images/characters/2026/01/f268712e6a664f419a36f71b36b625ed.png" alt="" class="inline-image" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;width: 201px; height: 350px; margin: 0 0.25em;" data-inline="true" /></div>


**You will have three training sessions per day with Sepir. During each training session, you must reach 10,000 SP to unlock 20<img src="/images/content/items/ui_item_Exp_OverPoint_1.png" alt="" class="inline-image" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;width: 30px; height: 30px; margin: -2px 0 0 0;" data-inline="true" /><span class="text-purple-600 dark:text-purple-400">over points</span>. Once unlocked, you may distribute these<img src="/images/content/items/ui_item_Exp_OverPoint_1.png" alt="" class="inline-image" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;width: 30px; height: 30px; margin: -2px 0 0 0;" data-inline="true" /><span class="text-purple-600 dark:text-purple-400">over points</span> to specific stats. Each stat has a maximum<img src="/images/content/items/ui_item_Exp_OverPoint_1.png" alt="" class="inline-image" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;width: 30px; height: 30px; margin: -2px 0 0 0;" data-inline="true" /><span class="text-purple-600 dark:text-purple-400">over points</span> cap, which increases every time you gain 100 levels.**
