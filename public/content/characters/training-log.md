# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Training Diary Overview</span>
**This content unlocks when your character reaches level 400 and allows you to gain additional growth points to level up your stats. This is done by obtaining <span class="text-purple-600 dark:text-purple-400">OP Points</span> every 100 levels and completing training with Sepir which increases the current level cap of your stats in the growth points section.**
