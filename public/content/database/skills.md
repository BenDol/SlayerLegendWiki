# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Skill Database</span>

Every skill in the wiki's database - 46 of them - with the numbers that matter: element, MP cost, cooldown, level cap, and the effect with its base value and per-level growth. This data is the same dataset that powers the wiki's [Skill Builder](/skill-builder), so what you plan there matches what you read here. "Base" is the skill's value at level 1; each level adds the amount in parentheses. Damage actives cap at level 130, most buffs at 20.

For icons and a visual browse, see the [Skills Gallery](/skills/skills); for how to combine these into builds, start at the [Skills overview](/skills).

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Immortal Skills</span>

| Skill | Element | MP | CD | Max Lv | Effect |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| **Rave** | - | 50 | 60s | 5 | Echoes damage dealt to one enemy over 5s as bonus damage. Base 70% (+10%/Lv) |
| **Mantra** | - | 0 | - | 5 | Total ATK and HP up - works without occupying a skill slot. Base +30% (+10%/Lv) |

A note on the two Immortals, because they break the usual rules: **Rave** records the damage you deal in a 5-second window and releases a percentage of it back as bonus damage, which makes it multiply with every buff you can stack into that window (the community pairs it with Wrath of Gods and Warrior Burn for exactly this reason). And **Mantra** applies its effect *without being equipped in a slot* - a straight account power bump once leveled.
