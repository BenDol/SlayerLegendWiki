Slayer Legend Wiki is a community-maintained guide for **Slayer Legend: Idle RPG** (슬레이어 키우기). Whether you are just starting out or pushing end-game content, you will find [beginner guides](/getting-started), an in-depth [progression walkthrough](/andy/overview-introduction), and full references covering [skills](/skills), [equipment](/equipment), [spirits](/spirits), [companions](/companions), and [stages](/stages).

If you haven't played it: Slayer Legend is a pixel-art idle RPG from GEAR2 in which you raise a single sword-swinging Slayer from a nobody in the Beginning Forest to something that clears screens of monsters across **2,000 stages**. Your character fights on their own; your job is deciding where the earnings go. That sounds simple, and for the first hundred stages it is - then the systems start stacking. Twenty-four [promotion ranks](/character/promotions) that multiply your stats permanently. A twenty-grade [class system](/character/class) with awakenings beyond it. Forty-six [skills](/database/skills), eighty-one [soul weapons](/equipment/soul-weapons), twelve [spirits](/spirits/information), four [companions](/companions) with their own dice-rolled stat pages, twelve [relics](/equipment/relics). The game rarely explains how these layers interact. That's the gap this wiki exists to fill.

The wiki also hosts free interactive tools: plan your build in the [Skill Builder](/skill-builder), assemble your team in the [Spirit Builder](/spirit-builder), lay out [Soul Weapon engravings](/soul-weapon-engraving), and save or share complete [Battle Loadouts](/battle-loadouts) with other players.

## What Makes This Wiki Different

Two things, mainly. First, the reference pages and the tools run on the **same data** - the numbers you browse on the [Spirit Information](/spirits/information) page are the numbers the [Spirit Builder](/spirit-builder) calculates with, contributed and corrected by players who checked them in-game. When a patch shifts a value, fixing it once fixes it everywhere. Second, the guides are opinionated. Plenty of sources will tell you what a promotion *is*; the pages here tell you which multiplier to chase next, where your diamonds should actually go, and which daily habits compound - the kind of advice that normally lives buried in Discord scroll-back. [Andy's progression guide](/andy/overview-introduction), written from three years of play across two servers, anchors the strategy side, and the wiki's own [system guides](/guides) carry it the rest of the way.

## A Wiki Anyone Can Edit

Every page here is a plain markdown file that any player can improve. Sign in with GitHub, hit edit, and your change goes through the same review pipeline as everything else - contributions are checked before they publish, so the reference stays trustworthy even though it's open. Found a number that doesn't match your game? That's not a reason to distrust the page; it's an invitation to [fix it](/meta/contributing). The players who maintain this wiki play the game daily, and the whole project works because readers keep becoming editors.

## Getting Started

New to the game or the wiki? These are good first stops:

- [Getting Started](/getting-started) - the essentials for your first days in the game
- [Andy's Progression Guide](/andy/overview-introduction) - efficient progression for F2P and light spenders
- Use search (press `Ctrl+K`) to quickly find any page, item, or skill
- Sign in with GitHub to edit pages, submit guides, and contribute to the wiki

And if you just want to know what to do *right now*: follow the guide quests, put your gold into ATK, and read the [Early Game Roadmap](/getting-started/early-game-roadmap) before you spend a single diamond. Future-you will be grateful.
