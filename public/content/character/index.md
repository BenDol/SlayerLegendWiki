# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Your Slayer</span>

Every system in this section answers the same question: how do I make my Slayer hit harder and survive longer? The answer is layered. Your base stats are the foundation, and then promotions, classes, latent power, and the training diary each stack their own multipliers and bonuses on top. Understanding how the layers interact is most of what "getting good" at Slayer Legend means - the game rarely demands reflexes, but it constantly rewards knowing which layer is currently your weakest.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">The Growth Systems</span>

- **[Stats](/character/stats)** - the foundation. Gold-leveled combat stats (ATK, HP, HP Recovery, the critical family), growth points from leveling, Growing Knowledge trials, and the promotion ability slots. This is where the overwhelming majority of your gold goes, forever.
- **[Promotions](/character/promotions)** - one-time rank battles that permanently multiply ATK and HP. From Bronze's x2 up to Eldenwood's x42,000,000. When you hit a stage wall, check here first - the wall is usually a promotion you haven't taken.
- **[Class](/character/class)** - unlocked at stage 120 and level 250. Twenty summonable classes plus secret classes through awakening, each a further ATK/HP multiplier, with the Memory Tree layering resource and stat bonuses on top at the high end.
- **[Latent Power](/character/latent-power)** - Dragon Power from the Shelter of Sleeping Flame fuels randomized stat upgrades across five pages, and awakened latent power (from the Dragonos promotion onward) adds a grade-and-star ignition system above that.
- **[Training Diary](/character/training-diary)** - unlocked at level 400. Three daily training sessions with Sepir that grant Over Points and raise your growth-point stat caps. Small daily effort, large cumulative payoff.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">How the Layers Stack</span>

A rough mental model: stats are your base numbers, and everything else multiplies them. A promotion that doubles ATK doubles the value of every gold coin you ever spent on ATK - which is why taking promotions on time matters more than squeezing out a few thousand extra stat levels. Classes multiply again. Latent power and the training diary push the base itself higher. When you're stuck, the question is never "should I grind more gold" - it's "which multiplier is lagging."

If you want a guided path through all of this rather than a reference, the [Early Game Roadmap](/getting-started/early-game-roadmap) covers the unlock order, and [Andy's Progression Guide](/andy/overview-introduction) covers how veterans sequence these systems deep into the late game.
