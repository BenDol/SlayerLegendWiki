# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">The Twelve Spirits</span>

Every spirit in the game, organized by element. Effect ranges show the skill's span from low development to maximum - where a range reads "10-80%", your actual value depends on the spirit's tier and level. For how tiers, combining, and upgrades work, see the [Spirits overview](/spirits); to watch any of them animate through their 8 evolution stages, the [Spirit Viewer](/spirits/viewer) has the full gallery.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Reading the Table Strategically</span>

Notice how cleanly the twelve sort into jobs. The **economy trio** - Todd, Luga, and (indirectly) Zappy - exists to make farming pay: gold doubled, EXP doubled, kill times shortened. The **normal-monster crew** - Mum, Radon, Kart, Zappy again - accelerates stage clearing, and Radon's "above 70% HP" condition is quietly brilliant on a strong account, because when you one-shot monsters, every hit lands while they're above 70%. The **boss squad** - Noah, Loar, Sala, with Ark's time freeze as the wildcard - is built for timed promotion fights, where Noah's final-five-seconds multiplier lines up exactly with the moment you need it most. And **Bo** stands alone as pure survivability, the spirit you equip when a fight is killing you rather than out-scaling you.

The standard advice follows directly: run the farming set (Mum, Radon, Zappy, Luga, Todd rotating through your slots) for daily play, swap to Noah/Loar/Sala for promotion attempts, and consider Herh if your build leans on long-cooldown skills. Since only equipped spirits function and you'll have three slots at most, the swap itself is the skill - build both lineups in the [Spirit Builder](/spirit-builder) and change with the content, not out of habit.

Values shift with patches and community measurement - if your in-game numbers disagree with a range here, [edit this page](/meta/contributing) and set the record straight.
