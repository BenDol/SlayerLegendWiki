# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Spirits Overview</span>

Spirits are the small floating allies with outsized consequences. There are **twelve** of them - three per element, Fire, Water, Wind, and Earth - and each carries exactly one skill. But what skills: stopping time, executing monsters below an HP threshold, doubling your gold, doubling your EXP, +500% total HP. A spirit isn't a stat stick; it's a *rule change*. Choosing which rules apply to your account is what the spirit system actually is.

The catch that governs everything: **only equipped spirits provide their benefits.** Equip slots are scarce - unlocking the full set of three costs a cumulative 15,000<img src="/images/content/goods/Goods_Emerald.png" alt="" class="inline-image" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;width: 30px; height: 30px; margin: -2px 0 0 0;" data-inline="true" />**Emeralds** - earned from [companion advancement battles](/companions), which makes those battles even more worth doing promptly. Three slots, twelve spirits, and content-specific choices: that's the puzzle.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Tiers, Combining, and Upgrades</span>

Spirits come in tiers - **Common, Great, Rare, Epic, Legendary** - and combine upward the way half this game does: **three spirits of the same tier fuse into the next tier**. Skill effect ranges scale with development: Todd's double-gold chance runs from 10% up to 80%, Ark's time freeze from 4 seconds to 10, and so on across all twelve. Leveling runs deep (the upgrade track goes to level 400) and consumes<img src="/images/content/goods/EnchantCube 1_1.png" alt="" class="inline-image" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;width: 30px; height: 30px; margin: -2px 0 0 0;" data-inline="true" />**Enhance Cubes** and<img src="/images/content/goods/Goods_ManaCrystal.png" alt="" class="inline-image" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;width: 30px; height: 30px; margin: -2px 0 0 0;" data-inline="true" />**Mana Crystals**, with each spirit also evolving visually through **8 evolution stages** as it grows (see them animated in the [Spirit Viewer](/spirits/viewer)).

After Ether, spirit upgrades also feed your [skill mastery](/skills/skill-mastery) points, and each [companion's](/companions) late kit boosts their own element's spirit stats. The web, as always, connects.

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">The Two Classic Lineups</span>

The community sorts spirit choices into two loadouts, swapped per content:

- **Farming:** the economy spirits - **Todd** (2x gold chance), **Luga** (2x EXP chance), plus killers like **Zappy** (executes normal monsters below an HP threshold), **Mum** (skill damage vs normal monsters), and **Radon** (bonus damage against healthy enemies - which, when you one-shot everything, is *every* enemy).
- **Bossing:** **Noah** (up to 2x damage in the final 5 seconds - promotion fights are timed, do the math), **Loar** (skill damage vs bosses), and **Sala** or **Ark** depending on the fight (percent-HP burn versus a mid-fight time freeze).

Full details on all twelve - elements, skills, exact ranges - live on the [Spirit Information](/spirits/information) page.
