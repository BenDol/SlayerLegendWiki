# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">Spirit Viewer</span>

The Spirit Viewer is one of the wiki's interactive tools: a full animated gallery of all **12 spirits** across every one of their **8 evolution stages**. Each spirit's pixel art changes as it evolves - sometimes subtly, sometimes into something you'd barely recognize from stage one - and the viewer lets you browse idle, attack, and movement animations for any spirit at any stage, with playback controls for speed and animation type.

It's partly a practical tool and partly, let's be honest, just a nice thing to look at. The practical part: if you're deciding which spirits to develop, seeing the evolution stages tells you what you're working toward, and the viewer doubles as a quick visual reference when someone in the community mentions a spirit by look rather than name ("the little flaming one" is Sala, for the record).

If this page loaded as plain text rather than the interactive viewer, your browser may still be loading the tool - it lives right here at [/spirits/viewer](/spirits/viewer).

# <span class="text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100">About Evolution Stages</span>

A spirit's evolution stage is tied to its development - as you upgrade a spirit through its tiers and levels, its appearance advances through the eight stages, growing from a small flickering creature into its fully realized form. The viewer shows all eight side by side, which makes it easy to see how far along your own spirits are and what the next milestone looks like. Sprite animations run at the game's native frame counts, so what you see here is exactly what you'll see in battle.
