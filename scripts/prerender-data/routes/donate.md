# Donate

Slayer Legend Wiki is free, has no paywalls, and stays that way. Donations are the optional part: they cover hosting and development costs for the site and its interactive build tools.

Donating happens through PayPal, with preset amounts starting at five dollars or a custom amount of your choosing. If you include your GitHub username, a Donator badge appears next to your name across the wiki - a small thank-you visible wherever you contribute.

No pressure either way. Editing a page, submitting a build, or fixing a wrong number on the [contributing page's](/meta/contributing) checklist supports the wiki just as genuinely as money does.
