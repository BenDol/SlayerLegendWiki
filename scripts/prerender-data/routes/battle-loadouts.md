# Battle Loadouts

Battle Loadouts is the everything-planner: one page that holds a complete Slayer Legend setup, mirroring the game's Battle Settings screen. A single loadout carries your ten-slot skill bar, your three-spirit lineup, your familiar trio with its prime, your skill stone refinement, and your soul weapon engraving grid - so when you ask "here's my whole build, what should I change?", the answer can actually see the whole build.

## What you can do

Each section opens its matching builder in a popup - the same [Skill Builder](/skill-builder), [Spirit Builder](/spirit-builder), [Familiar Builder](/familiar-builder), [Skill Stone Builder](/skill-stone-builder), and [Soul Weapon Engraving](/soul-weapon-engraving) tools that exist as standalone pages, just embedded here. Configure a section, save it back to the loadout, move to the next. Skills and spirits can be dragged between slots directly on the loadout page, and clicking any equipped skill shows its full details.

Give the loadout a name and, signed in with GitHub, save it to your account - you can keep several and swap between them. Any loadout shares as one link that reproduces the entire configuration, or exports and imports as a JSON file. Drafts auto-save in your browser while you build.

## Tips

- Build the individual pieces in the dedicated builders first if you prefer working small; anything you've saved there loads straight into a loadout.
- Keep separate loadouts per content type - farming, bossing, promotion pushes - rather than editing one loadout back and forth.
- Asking for build advice on the community Discord? Share the loadout link instead of screenshots. One link, every decision visible.
