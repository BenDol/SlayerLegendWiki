# Content Creators

This page gathers the community's Slayer Legend creators in one place: approved streamers with live Twitch and YouTube channels embedded when they're broadcasting, plus a library of video guides you can actually search.

Guides are filterable by category - beginner, intermediate, advanced, farming, promotion - and by difficulty, so "show me an advanced promotion guide" is a two-click question rather than a YouTube rabbit hole. Every video links back to its creator.

The library is community-fed. Anyone can submit a video guide, and streamers can apply to have their channel listed; submissions go through review before appearing, which keeps the page useful rather than merely long. Found a video that taught you something the wiki's written guides didn't? Submit it - the next player searching for that exact answer will find it here.

Prefer reading to watching? The [guides section](/guides) covers the same ground in text.
