# Soul Weapon Engraving Planner

Engraving is the grid mini-game that opens at stage 320 with the soul weapon Pride: tetris-style soul gem pieces dropped as you play, a unique grid per weapon, and a completion bonus for filling every slot with no gaps. In-game, experimenting costs Chaos Souls - each reroll randomizes your pieces. This tool lets you solve the grid for free first.

## What you can do

Pick from 69 soul weapons, Pride onward, each with its real grid pattern and completion effect. Load your actual pieces into the eight inventory slots: seven shapes, each tied to a stat (L-shape carries ATK, reverse-L HP, T-shape HP Recovery, square Critical Damage, line Extra Gold, and the two squiggles Accuracy and Dodge), in six rarities from Common to Mythic with levels up to 50. Then drag pieces onto the grid - placement previews before you commit, and pieces rotate in 90-degree steps.

The clever parts: **Auto-Solve** searches your inventory for every complete fill of the current grid and lets you pick one. **Find Best Weapon** scans grids against your inventory to suggest where your pieces fit best, respecting the highest weapon you've actually unlocked. There are preset inventories for common endgame piece mixes, and community-submitted layouts per weapon - browse what other players run, or submit your own. Builds share as links, save to your GitHub account, and export as JSON.

## Tips

- Set your highest unlocked weapon first so suggestions stay realistic.
- Run Auto-Solve before spending a single Chaos Soul in-game.
- Piece priority, per the [soul weapons guide](/equipment/soul-weapons): ATK first, Critical Damage second, gold lines for farming setups.
