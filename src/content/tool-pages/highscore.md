# Contributor Highscore

This is the wiki's contributor leaderboard: everyone who has edited Slayer Legend Wiki pages, ranked by the size of their contributions across the wiki's GitHub repository. The top three get the podium; the list runs to the top hundred.

Three tabs split the rankings - All Time, This Month, and This Week - so a new contributor can climb a fresh board instead of chasing years of history. Scores update automatically on a schedule.

Want your name here? Every page on the wiki is editable - see [how to contribute](/meta/contributing). Fixing one wrong number counts.
