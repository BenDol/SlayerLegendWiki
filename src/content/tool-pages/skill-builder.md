# Skill Builder

The Skill Builder is a free planner for your Slayer Legend skill bar. It runs on the same database as the wiki's [skill reference](/database/skills) - all 46 skills with their grade, element, MP cost, and cooldown - so you can assemble and compare full bars without spending anything in-game.

## What you can do

Fill up to ten skill slots from the complete skill list, searching by name or filtering by element (Fire, Water, Wind, Earth) and grade (Common through Legendary). Each equipped skill gets its own enhancement level, adjustable per slot - or flip on auto max level to see what a build looks like at its ceiling. The slot count is adjustable too: if your account only has seven slots unlocked, plan with seven. Drag skills between slots to mirror the order you'd actually run.

When a build is ready, share it as a short link that reproduces the exact bar for anyone who opens it, or export it as a JSON file for safekeeping. Sign in with GitHub to save builds to your account (they appear under [My Collections](/my-collections)); without signing in, your work-in-progress auto-saves as a draft in your browser, so a closed tab doesn't cost you the build.

## Tips

- Keep your farming preset and your boss preset as two separate saves - the strongest bar for each barely overlaps.
- Set the slot count to what you've genuinely unlocked before comparing builds. A ten-slot fantasy bar tells you nothing about your next upgrade.
- Skill choice depends on more than the bar itself - the [skills overview](/skills) covers mastery and refinement, which decide how these picks perform.
