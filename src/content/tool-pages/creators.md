# Content Creators

This page is where the community's Slayer Legend creators will be listed: streamers with Twitch and YouTube channels, and video guides organised by category (beginner, intermediate, advanced, farming, promotion) and by difficulty. Entries go through a review before they appear, so the page only ever lists creators and guides someone has checked.

The list is community-fed. If you make Slayer Legend videos, or you've learned something from one that the wiki's written guides don't cover, submit it here - the submission forms are on this page, and approved entries link back to their creators.

Prefer reading to watching? The [guides section](/guides) covers the same ground in text, starting with the [Early Game Roadmap](/getting-started/early-game-roadmap) and [Andy's progression guide](/andy/overview-introduction).
