# Spirit Builder

The Spirit Builder plans your three [spirit](/spirits) equip slots - the scarcest real estate in Slayer Legend, since only equipped spirits provide their benefits and there are twelve spirits competing for three places. The tool carries the full roster, three per element across Fire, Water, Wind, and Earth, each with its skill and effect ranges visible while you build.

## What you can do

Pick a spirit for each of the three slots, then configure that slot the way your account actually looks: spirit level, awakening level, evolution stage, and skill enhancement level are all set independently per slot. Drag spirits between slots to reorder. If you've saved custom spirits to your personal collection, you can pull those in directly instead of starting from the base roster.

Finished lineups share as a short link that reproduces the exact configuration, or export as JSON files. Signed in with GitHub, you can save named builds to your account and load them back any time from [My Collections](/my-collections); signed out, a draft auto-saves in your browser as you work.

## Tips

- Build the two classic lineups as separate saves: a farming set (economy spirits like Todd and Luga plus executioners) and a boss set (Noah, Loar, and a fight-dependent third). The [spirits overview](/spirits) explains why they don't mix.
- Check each spirit's effect range before committing - skill values scale hard with development, and a spirit at low investment is a different spirit.
- Want to see your picks animate through their evolution stages? The [Spirit Viewer](/spirits/viewer) shows all twelve.
